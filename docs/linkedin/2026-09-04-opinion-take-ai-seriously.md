Most of my working day now goes into briefing AI coding agents and checking what they hand back, rather than writing the code myself. That shift took months, and it hasn't settled.

So I don't read this as another tooling cycle we absorb and move on from. Earlier waves took over the mechanical part of the work. In my own work, this one is taking over the part I'd have called judgment. Plenty of people I trust read it the other way, and they may be right.

I'd rather act as if it's real and keep learning. If I'm wrong, I've spent the time getting better at my job. If I'm not, I'll be glad I started early.

Not a warning, just one person's read: I think this is worth taking seriously.
