## Decisions

- **Purix lives in the Apps group, not a new one.** It is a shipped end-user product, and it does ship Safari targets for macOS and iOS; the group label absorbs "browser" rather than the site growing a fourth group for one card.
- **The yard is a ratchet now, and that is folded into `AGENTS.md`.** Per-repo numbers become "the largest transcript window ever observed" rather than an exact all-time sum — the honest trade for a building that never disappears. The site-wide totals and daily series remain genuinely all-time. A repo can no longer fall out of the archive, so an allowlisted repo with no `YARD` slot now fails the build until someone adds one, instead of self-healing after 30 days.
- **Nameplate positions are verified by sampling the whole badge disc in a browser**, not by one `elementFromPoint` at its centre — the centre test passes over a 0.5px crane hairline that visibly strikes through the digits, which is how the first attempt at Purix's badge got through. Four `np` values changed under that check.
- **`.fy-cards--lead4`** added to `global.css` + `WorkGroup.astro`. The existing lead grid spans followers across 2 of 6 columns, which fits 1+3 exactly; 1+4 stranded the fourth card at a third of the row. The modifier spans followers across 3 columns for a 2x2, and applies only when a lead group has exactly five projects.
